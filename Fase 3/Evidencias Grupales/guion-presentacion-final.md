### Lámina 3 · El problema / The problem
**Habla Jorge · en inglés · 1 min 5 s · al terminar 1:45.**

> Let me start with the problem. In Chile, about one and a half million adults live with dependency, according to the 2022 national disability and dependency survey. Almost all of the people who care for them at home, 96.4 percent, receive no pay, and seven out of ten are women. 41.3 percent of these adults have no formal caregiver at all. ### Lámina 4 · El diagnóstico / The root cause
**Habla Jorge · en inglés · 55 s · al terminar 2:40 · pasa la palabra a Martín.**

> When we looked closer, we found two problems that seem different but are really the same one. The first is continuity. **Nota en español:** dos problemas que son el mismo, la continuidad y el abastecimiento, porque el cuidado depende de una persona que no puede delegar. Nueve de cada diez cuidadoras entrevistadas dejaron de salir por falta de relevo. En la lámina el título en inglés es «The root cause».

### Lámina 5 · La solución / The solution
**Habla Martín, recibe la palabra de Jorge · en inglés · 1 min 25 s · al terminar 4:05.**

> Thank you, Jorge. MATU has three layers, and the order matters. Layer one is the product: a relief sheet with only three fields. Their day, what to do if they get upset, and who to call. The caregiver shares it with the person who replaces her through a link and a PIN. The link expires on its own, and she can cut off access at any time. Layer two is what makes us different. From the same care routine, the family records how much of each supply the person uses, and MATU predicts the date each one will run out and builds the replenishment list. Layer three is delivery: a catalogue, an order and a single payment through Transbank. It is a service, not the reason MATU exists. If we switch it off, the family keeps its sheet, its forecast and its list, and can buy anywhere. Why this order? Because the forecast comes from knowing the person. Nobody outside the family knows that she uses four diapers a day. And MATU never asks for clinical information. It asks how to care for someone, not what illness they have.

**Nota en español:** las tres capas y su orden. La ficha de relevo es el producto, la predicción de quiebre es el diferenciador y el despacho es un servicio que se puede quitar. MATU no pide datos clínicos.

### Lámina 13 · Arquitectura de la aplicación / Solution architecture
**Habla Martín · en inglés · 1 min 25 s · al terminar 10:40.**

> Now, the architecture. MATU is a modular monolith: one FastAPI application with ten modules, such as identity, care plan, consumption, ordering and payments. Dependencies always point downwards, and an automated test fails the pipeline if a module breaks that rule. That is why the care plan does not know that delivery exists, and why layer three can be removed: twenty-one tests check that the sheet and the forecast keep working without it. The data lives in PostgreSQL 16, with Row Level Security forced on every business table. Each family is isolated by the database itself, so a query without a family context returns zero rows, and the application connects with a role that cannot bypass those rules. The sheet is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, with one key per cared-for person, so deleting the key erases the data. The web app is built with React and TypeScript, designed first for a small phone screen. Payments go through Webpay Plus, and MATU never sees card data. Finally, we designed for Law 21.719, which comes fully into force in December.

**Nota en español:** monolito modular de diez módulos con dependencias hacia abajo, verificadas por una prueba. La capa 3 se puede quitar (21 pruebas de MATU-40). Aislamiento por fila forzado en la base, cifrado con una clave por persona, web mobile first, Webpay sin datos de tarjeta y diseño para la Ley 21.719.
